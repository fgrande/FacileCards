Your cards, right away.

FacileCards keeps your store loyalty cards on your phone and shows them at the checkout in a moment, even offline.

  • Fast. Open the app, tap the card, the code is on screen.
  • All on your phone. No account, no sign-up: your cards stay on the device.
  • Organised your way. Your own sections, sorted by name, date added or last use.
  • Shareable. Send a section or a single card to anyone, through WhatsApp or any other app.

Why FacileCards

FacileCards comes from a simple need: a fast, easy app for showing your loyalty cards in shops.

When I get to the checkout, I don’t want to hold up the queue because my phone isn’t responding. I want the card on screen in the shortest possible time.

That is why FacileCards doesn’t depend on the network: your cards live on your phone, so there is no connection to wait for and no lag. There are ads, but they are not intrusive.
