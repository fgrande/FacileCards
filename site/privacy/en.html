Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

FacileCards does not collect your data: cards, codes and photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded to any server or cloud of the developer.

Controller

Fabio Grande, developer of the FacileCards app.
Contact: grnfba@gmail.com

Data that stays on your device

The cards you add, their codes, names, notes, sections and any photos, your name (optional, suggested as the holder of new cards) and your settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else: the app has no account, no server and no sign-up.

Cards you delete stay in the app’s bin for 30 days and are then erased; you can also delete them permanently straight away.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device. Copies you made yourself remain (exported files, wherever you saved or sent them), as does any Android backup in your Google account until you delete it.

Internet

The app does not need the internet to work: your cards open even without a connection. It connects to the internet only to show ads and to check the Pro purchase with Google Play.

Camera and gallery

The app asks for camera access only to read a card’s code or to photograph the card. You can also read a code from a photo in your gallery: the app sees only the photo you pick, not the rest of the gallery. Images stay on the device.

Payment app

If you like, you can open a payment app (for example Satispay or Google Wallet) from the card with one tap. To suggest suitable apps, FacileCards checks on your phone whether some well-known payment apps are installed and which apps declare that they can pay by holding the phone near the card reader. It does not see the other apps on your phone, and this information never leaves the device.

Sharing and backup

When you export a section, a card or the whole archive, the app creates a file and hands it to the application you choose (for example WhatsApp, email or a storage service). From then on the file is handled by that application under its own terms.

The file contains the card codes, with names, holders, notes and photos: whoever receives it can use the cards. If you do not protect it with a password, anyone who gets the file can read its content. If you choose a password, the content is encrypted and the password is needed to open it; the password is not stored anywhere, so if you forget it nobody can recover it, not even the developer. A file with a single section or card does not contain favourites, usage dates or your name; the full copy of all your cards does, because it is used to restore them.

When you open a file you received with FacileCards (for example from WhatsApp, email or Drive), the app reads it only on your phone; the imported cards become yours and stay on the device like the others.

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, the app’s data (cards, sections, photos and settings) may be included in your Google account backup and in the transfer of data to a new phone. The backup is made by the phone and managed by Google, not by the developer. You can always export all your data to a file and keep it wherever you like.

Ads

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show them, Google may process data such as the device advertising identifier, IP address and information about how the app is used. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent at first launch; you can change it at any time in the app settings. If you do not consent, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

The Pro version shows no ads.

Purchases

The purchase of the Pro version is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your card details or any other payment data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on the developer’s side: everything is on your device and under your control. For data processed by Google, Google’s own tools and policies apply. For any question, write to the contact above.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
