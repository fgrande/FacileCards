Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

FacileCards does not collect your data: cards, codes and photos stay on your phone.

Controller

Fabio Grande, developer of the FacileCards app.
Contact: grnfba@gmail.com

Data that stays on your device

The cards you add, their codes, names, sections and any photos are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else: the app has no account, no server and no sign-up.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device.

Camera

The app asks for camera access only to read a card’s code or to photograph the card. Images stay on the device.

Sharing and backup

When you export a section, a card or the whole archive, the app creates a file and hands it to the application you choose (for example WhatsApp, email or a storage service). From then on the file is handled by that application under its own terms. The file contains card codes in clear text: whoever receives it can use them.

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, the app’s data (cards, sections and photos) may be included in your Google account backup. The backup is made by the phone and managed by Google, not by the developer. You can always export all your data to a file and keep it wherever you like.

Ads

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show them, Google may process data such as the device advertising identifier, IP address and information about how the app is used. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent at first launch; you can change it at any time in the app settings. If you do not consent, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

The Pro version shows no ads.

Purchases

The purchase of the Pro version is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your card details or any other payment data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on the developer’s side: everything is on your device and under your control. For data processed by Google, Google’s own tools and policies apply. For any question, write to the contact above.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
